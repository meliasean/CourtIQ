# CourtIQ

A tennis match prediction and value analysis system for ATP tour-level matches.

## Overview

CourtIQ combines a calibrated match-outcome model with a proprietary selection filter to identify tour-level matches where the model detects meaningful pricing edge relative to the market.

The system produces two layers of output:

- **Match predictions** — probability estimates for every scheduled match at tracked tournaments, generated by a random-forest classifier over player form, surface-adjusted ELO, head-to-head history, and other match-level features. The model is trained on ATP match data going back multiple seasons and calibrated against consensus market pricing to correct for well-known probability biases.

- **Value picks** — a filtered subset of those matches, selected by a proprietary scoring formula that weighs model-market edge, prediction confidence, cross-signal agreement, and volatility. The formula assigns each match to one of several conviction buckets and excludes the majority of matches as having no exploitable edge.

## What's public, what isn't

The site at [meliasean.github.io/tennis-match-predictor](https://meliasean.github.io/tennis-match-predictor) shows all predictions and all picks, timestamped before matches play, with results scored honestly afterward. Nothing is filtered, curated, or removed once posted.

What isn't public: the specific formula weights, bucket thresholds, and override rules used to filter matches. These are the result of significant research and represent the core intellectual property of the project. They are not present in this repository.

## Methodology at a glance

- **Predictions** use a random-forest classifier calibrated via isotonic regression.
- **Selection** applies a multi-factor scoring formula (details private) that combines model probability, market-implied probability, ELO-derived signals, and other quantitative factors.
- **Buckets** categorize picks by conviction level; heavy-chalk favorites and low-edge matches are excluded regardless of model confidence.
- **Live tracking** logs every non-excluded pick pre-match, with outcomes scored from published results after each match completes.
- **The formula is locked** for the duration of the current live measurement period. No mid-stream adjustments are made, so the resulting track record reflects the honest behavior of the formula rather than post-hoc curation.
